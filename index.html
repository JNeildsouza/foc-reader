A space for your attention.

1. Bring a document. Upload PDF, Word, or text. Review the extracted text, then enter your studio. Pasted text is kept unchanged.

2. Choose a voice. Kokoro offers natural male voices and runs in a browser worker after its first download. System voices start without a model download; some device voices may use their provider’s online service.

3. Set your pace. Choose a speed and comfort limit. Turn on passage spotlight or pauses between paragraphs. Click a word to jump, or press Space to play and pause.

Word timing. Kokoro highlights use estimated word timings within each generated sentence. System voices use word boundaries when the browser provides them, otherwise estimated timings. Text extraction and spoken pronunciation can differ from the visual source; review numbers and complex layouts.

Your library. Text and reading positions are saved only in this browser. Clearing browser data removes them. Use the download button in your library to keep a text copy. This app does not sync documents across devices.

Install. Use your browser’s Install app or Add to Home Screen command. On iPhone, open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The app shell works offline after one online visit; AI voices need a complete cached model and runtime.
